# 💰 Glyde — Business Model & Monetisation

---

## Overview

Glyde operates a dual-sided marketplace model with two distinct revenue streams that reinforce each other.

- **B2C (Attendees):** Free to use. Revenue comes from transaction commissions and optional premium features.
- **B2B (Venues & Vendors):** Paid subscription per event or annually. Revenue from platform fees, analytics, and premium tools.

The two sides feed each other: venues pay because their attendees use it, and attendees use it because their venues offer it. Every venue you sign is automatically a B2C distribution channel.

---

## B2C Revenue Streams

### 1. Food & Drink Order Commission
- **How it works:** Glyde takes 5–10% of every food and drink order placed through the app
- **Who pays:** The vendor (deducted before payout, invisible to attendee)
- **Why vendors accept it:** They sell more by removing queues and reaching attendees who wouldn't have queued
- **Estimated value:** At a 5,000-person event, if 30% of attendees place one £12 order, that's £18,000 in GMV. At 8% commission = £1,440 per event

### 2. Merchandise Commission
- **How it works:** Same as food — 5–10% of every merch order placed through the app
- **Who pays:** The merch vendor
- **Why it works:** Glyde makes it easier to discover and buy merch — vendors sell more overall

### 3. Premium Passport [Claude Suggestion]
- **How it works:** £2.99 per event unlocks bonus XP multipliers, exclusive badges, priority map features
- **Who pays:** The attendee
- **Why they pay:** Competitive XP earners want an edge on the leaderboard
- **Alternative model:** Annual subscription (£9.99/year) for power users who attend multiple events

### 4. Sponsored Quests [Claude Suggestion]
- **How it works:** Brands (Red Bull, Heineken, Spotify, etc.) pay Glyde to be featured as a quest
- **Example:** "Visit the Red Bull stand and scan the QR to earn 500 XP"
- **Who pays:** The brand or sponsor
- **Why brands pay:** Direct, measurable engagement with event attendees — better ROI than a banner at the venue
- **Estimated value:** £500–£5,000 per quest per event depending on attendance and brand

### 5. Ride Home Commission [Claude Suggestion]
- **How it works:** Affiliate partnership with Uber, Bolt, or local taxi apps
- **In-app:** "Book your ride home" button when user leaves the venue
- **Commission:** £1–£3 per completed ride referral
- **Why it works:** Attendees need a ride home — Glyde is perfectly placed to offer it at the right moment

### 6. Data Insights (Anonymised, Aggregated) [Claude Suggestion]
- **How it works:** Aggregate, fully anonymised data sold to event industry research firms
- **What it includes:** Movement patterns, peak times, most popular POIs, ordering behaviour
- **Who buys it:** Festival industry associations, venue planning consultancies, brand agencies
- **Important:** Never individual data. Always aggregated. GDPR compliant.
- **Estimated value:** £500–£2,000 per dataset, sold quarterly

## B2B Revenue Streams

### 1. Per-Event Platform Fee
The primary revenue stream. Venues pay per event.

| Event Size | Attendance | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Small | Up to 2,500 | £300 |
| Medium | 2,500 – 10,000 | £800 |
| Large | 10,000 – 25,000 | £2,000 |
| Major | 25,000 – 50,000 | £4,500 |
| Festival | 50,000+ | Custom quote |

**What's included in all tiers:**
- Venue map setup by Glyde team
- Unlimited attendees
- Full attendee app access
- Reports dashboard
- Vendor order management
- Basic analytics

### 2. Annual SaaS Licence [Claude Suggestion]
- For venues that run multiple events per year
- Annual fee replaces per-event fee at a discount
- Includes priority support and dedicated account manager

| Tier | Events/Year | Annual Fee | Saving vs Per-Event |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | Up to 6 events | £2,000/year | ~30% |
| Pro | Up to 20 events | £5,500/year | ~40% |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | £12,000/year | 50%+ |

### 3. Analytics Add-On
- Premium analytics dashboard with deeper insights
- Post-event PDF report
- Comparison across events
- Staff performance metrics
- **Additional cost:** £150–£500 per event or included in Pro/Enterprise tier

### 4. White-Label App [Claude Suggestion]
- Large festivals or recurring events want their own branded app
- Glyde powers the backend, but the app shows the client's logo, colours, and name
- **Pricing:** £8,000–£25,000 setup fee + annual licence
- **Target:** Glastonbury-scale events, sports stadium operators, large venue chains

### 5. Vendor Onboarding Fee [Claude Suggestion]
- One-off setup fee per vendor stall
- Covers menu upload, payment integration, and training
- **Pricing:** £50–£150 per vendor stall
- **At a large festival with 40 vendors:** £2,000–£6,000 additional revenue per event

### 6. Hardware Partnership [Claude Suggestion]
- Partner with tablet providers for pre-configured Glyde vendor tablets
- Rent or sell tablets to vendors who don't have their own device
- **Pricing:** £10–£20/day rental per tablet
- At a 40-vendor festival running 3 days: up to £2,400 additional revenue

## Financial Projections (Conservative Estimates)

### Year 1 — Prove it works
- 5 pilot events (3 free, 2 paid)
- 2 paid events at average £500 each = **£1,000**
- Food orders at 2 paid events: 500 orders × £10 × 8% = **£400**
- **Year 1 total: ~£1,400**
- Goal: Case studies, not revenue

### Year 2 — Start selling
- 24 paid events across the year
- Average event fee: £800 = **£19,200**
- Food/merch commission across all events: **£8,000**
- 2 annual licences: **£4,000**
- Sponsored quests: **£3,000**
- **Year 2 total: ~£34,200**

### Year 3 — Scale
- 60 events + 8 annual licences
- Platform fees: **£48,000**
- Commissions: **£25,000**
- Annual licences: **£16,000**
- Premium Passport and quests: **£12,000**
- **Year 3 total: ~£101,000**

> These are conservative. A single white-label deal or major festival partnership could significantly change these numbers.

## Pricing Strategy

**Start free, prove value, then charge.**

The first 3-5 venues get Glyde completely free. This is not a loss — it is investment in case studies, testimonials, and product validation. The first paying customer will pay because they have seen the proof.

**Don't compete on price.** Glyde does something no competitor does today. Price it for the value it delivers, not the cost to build it. A venue that runs a 10,000-person festival and gets a detailed analytics report, reduced complaints, and increased food order revenue will happily pay £2,000 for the platform.

## Payment Technology

**Stripe** is the recommended payment processor for all in-app transactions.

- Supports card payments, Apple Pay, Google Pay
- Built-in marketplace payments (split between vendor and Glyde)
- Handles payouts to multiple vendors automatically
- Strong fraud protection
- Available in 40+ countries
- Developer-friendly API

**Flow:**
```
Attendee pays £12 for food
→ Stripe processes payment
→ Stripe fee (~£0.30 + 1.4%): ~£0.47
→ Glyde commission (8%): £0.96
→ Vendor receives: £10.57
→ Payout to vendor: after event or weekly
```
